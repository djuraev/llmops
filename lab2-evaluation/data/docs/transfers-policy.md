# Transfers policy (MoliyaBank, fictional)

## Card-to-card transfers

The daily limit for card-to-card transfers is 50,000,000 UZS per card. The monthly limit is 200,000,000 UZS per card. Transfers between MoliyaBank cards are free. Transfers to cards of other banks (Uzcard or Humo) have a fee of 0.5%.

## Cancelling a transfer and fees

Completed card-to-card transfers cannot be cancelled. If you sent money to the wrong card, open the app and go to Profile > Support > Wrong transfer; the bank reviews the request within 3 business days and a refund of the amount is not guaranteed. Transfer fees are never refunded once a transfer is completed, including when a request is made later.
