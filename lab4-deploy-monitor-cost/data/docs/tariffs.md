# Tariff plans (MoliyaBank, fictional)

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly fee | Cashback | Card reissue |
|------|-------------|----------|--------------|
| Start | 0 UZS | 0.5% on all purchases | 25,000 UZS |
| Premium | 49,000 UZS | 2% in cafes and restaurants, 0.5% elsewhere | free |

## Changing your plan

You can change your plan in the app: Settings > Plan. The new plan takes effect from the 1st day of the next month. Prices and discounts can only be changed by the bank's tariff committee; staff and chat assistants cannot offer other prices.
