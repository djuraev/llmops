# Branches (MoliyaBank, fictional)

## Opening hours

The Chilonzor branch – next to the metro café – is open 24/7 for card services. All other branches are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00–18:00. Customers can book a visit in the app: Branches › Book a visit. Closing an account requires a visit to any branch with a passport.
