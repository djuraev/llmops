# Premium plan (MoliyaBank, fictional)

## Premium plan price and benefits

The Premium plan costs 39,000 UZS per month. Premium customers get 1.5% cashback in cafes and restaurants and a free card reissue. You can switch to the Premium plan at any MoliyaBank branch, and the new plan takes effect immediately. Premium is our most popular plan for customers who often pay by card in cafes, restaurants and shops across Uzbekistan.
