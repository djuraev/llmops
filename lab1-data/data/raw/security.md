# Security guidance (MoliyaBank, fictional)

## SMS codes and passwords

MoliyaBank staff never ask for SMS codes, CVV codes, full card numbers or app passwords. Anyone asking for them is a fraudster, even if the caller ID shows the bank's number. Never share these codes.

## Example support transcript (staff training, all data fake)

Customer: Hello, my phone is +998 90 123 45 67 and my card is 8600 1234 5678 9012. My passport is AA1234567, PINFL 31234567890123, email aziz.karimov@example.uz. Why was my payment declined?
Agent: For your safety, please never send your full card number or passport details in chat. The payment was declined because the daily limit was reached.
