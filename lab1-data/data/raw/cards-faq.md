# Cards FAQ (MoliyaBank, fictional)

## Lost or stolen card

Block the card immediately in the app: Cards > select the card > Block. You can also call the 24/7 support line 1234. A blocked card cannot be unblocked; order a reissue instead.

## Card reissue

A new card is ready in 5 business days. Reissue costs 25,000 UZS on the Start plan and is free on the Premium plan.

## PIN code

The app cannot show your PIN code. You can change the PIN at any MoliyaBank ATM using your current PIN.
